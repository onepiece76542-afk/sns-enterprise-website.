Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SNS Enterprise ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact or order form, we collect the name, email address, phone number, and order details you provide. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Cookies

We use cookies as described in our Cookie Policy. Non-essential cookies (such as analytics) are only set after you accept the cookie banner.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to quote requests, process orders, and communicate with you about your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Form submissions are stored securely in our database/order system so our team can follow up with you.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
