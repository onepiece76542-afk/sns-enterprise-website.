Frequently Asked Questions

Where is SNS Enterprise located?

We are based at Hariyo Pool, Kathmandu, Bagmati Province 44600, Nepal. See the map on our About page.

Are your international products genuine?

Yes. International products are supplied only through the distributor officially selected by the original manufacturer for Nepal.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes, SNS Enterprise primarily supplies retailers and bulk buyers. Use the contact form to request a quote.

How do I place an order?

Fill out the form on our Contact / Order page with the products and quantities you need, and our team will follow up.

Do you deliver outside Kathmandu?

Please contact us with your location and order size so we can confirm delivery options.
