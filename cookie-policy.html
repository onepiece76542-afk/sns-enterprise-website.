Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website uses cookies -- small text files stored on your device -- to make the site work properly and, with your consent, to understand how visitors use it.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie choice). These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand site traffic and usage. These are only set if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.

Managing Cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown when you first visit this site. You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
