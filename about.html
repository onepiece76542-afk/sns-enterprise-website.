About SNS Enterprise

SNS Enterprise is a distribution company based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We supply retailers, shops, and bulk buyers with everyday grocery and household products -- including instant noodles, confectionery, dish and laundry cleaning products, and packaged beverages.

Products made in Nepal are purchased directly from the manufacturer. International products are supplied through the distributor that the original manufacturer has officially selected for Nepal, so every item we sell is authentic.
